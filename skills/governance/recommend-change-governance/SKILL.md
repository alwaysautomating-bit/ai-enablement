# Recommend Change Governance

1. Confirm the rigidity classification and unresolved dissent.
2. Match controls to consequence and reversibility.
3. Choose one path: direct iteration, bounded experiment, explicit decision, governed migration, or stop pending authority/evidence.
4. Define validation, rollback, communication, and approval requirements.
5. Preserve dissent that could change the outcome.

Do not grant approval or execution authority. Produce a recommendation with prerequisites, human gates, stop conditions, and residual risk.
